About Infralink

Built in Mumbai, engineered for growing businesses

Infralink Technologies started as a small team solving one recurring problem: businesses were hiring a web developer, an app developer, an IT guy and a security vendor separately — and none of them talked to each other.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a business website take?+

Most business websites take 3–5 weeks depending on complexity, number of pages, and feature requirements. We give you a firm timeline after the discovery call.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?+

Yes. Every plan includes a support window after launch, and Growth and Enterprise clients get priority or SLA-backed support for as long as they need it.

Can you work with our existing systems?+

Usually, yes. We regularly integrate with existing CRMs, payment gateways, ERPs and internal tools rather than asking you to replace everything at once.

Do you sign NDAs?+

Yes, we're happy to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion of your project or systems.

Do you only work with businesses in Mumbai?+

No — we're based in Mumbai but work remotely with clients across India and internationally, with on-site visits available by arrangement.
